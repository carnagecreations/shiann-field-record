If you've clicked through the dig sites on this record, you've met the mascots — an Ankylosaurus guarding Operations, a T. rex on Crisis & Support, a Maiasaura on Caregiving. People usually assume I picked them because they're fun. I did. But I also didn't pick them at random.

**Ankylosaurus** for Operations because it's armored and immovable — built to keep the whole structure standing no matter what hits it, which is exactly what operations work is supposed to do for everything happening around it.

**Maiasaura** for Caregiving because the name literally translates to "good mother lizard" — it's famous in the fossil record for nesting in colonies and raising hatchlings with real, documented care. Direct client care is the same instinct, just for people instead of dinosaurs.

**Troodon** for Training & Documentation because it had the largest brain-to-body ratio of any dinosaur we know of. Training work is fundamentally about transferring a working mental model into someone else's head efficiently — felt right.

**Deinonychus** for Web Design & Builder because it's fast, sharp, and built to execute — the smarter, bigger cousin of the velociraptor. Shipping software fast without cutting corners is the whole job.

**Tyrannosaurus rex** for Crisis & Support, obviously. The one you call when it's serious.

Every fossil photo linked on this site is a real museum specimen, credited properly under its Creative Commons license — not stock art. If you dig through all eight, you'll have unlocked the full collection down on the Basecamp page. That part's just for fun.
